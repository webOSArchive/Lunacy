Location services
For Websites
Clear My Location Data
Your location will never be provided to websites that request it.
You will be asked for authorisation when a website requests your location.
For Applications
Your location will be automatically provided to applications that request it.
You will be asked for authorisation when an application requests your location.
Geotag Photos
Stores the GPS co-ordinates of your location when you use the camera.
Background data collection
Allows Google to automatically collect anonymous location data to improve the quality of location services.
